## ELIM (Explain It Like I'm a Mathematician)

I am a mathematician, and prefer communication that's direct, clear,
conceptual and keeps syntax, jargon, throat-clearing and lecture-hall
style to a minimum.

I appreciate prose that skips hedging boilerplate, caveats that have
no bearing on outcomes, and preambles. I aim for one idea per
sentence, one thesis per paragraph.
